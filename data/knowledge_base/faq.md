# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How do I create an account?

To register, go to techstore.com and click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner. Enter your email, create a password (minimum 8 characters, including a number and an uppercase letter), and confirm your registration via the email sent to the provided address. You can also sign up using your Google or Apple account.

## How do I place an order?

Add the desired items to your cart, go to the "Cart" section, and click "Checkout." Select a shipping method, enter your address, choose a payment method, and confirm your order. After placing the order, you will receive an email with your order number and a tracking link.

## What payment methods are available?

We accept: credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank transfers (ACH), and cash on delivery (for courier deliveries). Buy Now, Pay Later options are available through Klarna and Afterpay. When paying online, your order is reserved immediately.

## How do I track my order?

After placing your order, you will receive a tracking number via email and SMS. You can track the status in your account under "My Orders" or via the direct link in the notification email. Statuses are updated in real time: "Processing," "Shipped," "In Transit," "Delivered."

## How do I contact support?

You can reach our support team through the following channels: live chat on the website (available 24/7), email at support@techstore.com (response within 2 hours during business hours), phone at 1-800-123-4567 (toll-free, Monday–Sunday 9 AM–9 PM ET), as well as via Telegram (@techstore_support) and WhatsApp.

## What are the warranty terms?

All electronics come with a manufacturer's warranty of 12 to 24 months depending on the product category. Warranty repairs are handled through authorized service centers. To file a warranty claim, you will need a receipt or electronic invoice from your account. Accessories have a 6-month warranty.

## What are the business hours?

The online store operates 24/7 — you can place orders at any time. Phone support is available Monday through Sunday from 9 AM to 9 PM Eastern Time. Live chat and email support operate 24/7. Pickup locations are open daily from 10 AM to 8 PM.

## Is there a loyalty program?

Yes, we have a rewards program called "TechRewards." You earn reward points on every purchase: 5% of the order total. Points can be used to cover up to 30% of your next order. 1 point = $0.01. Points are valid for 12 months from the date they are earned. Members also get early access to sales and exclusive coupons.

## How do I use a promo code?

Enter the promo code in the designated field on the checkout page ("Cart" → "Promo Code" field → "Apply" button). The discount will be reflected in the total. Promo codes cannot be combined with each other but can be used alongside reward points. Each promo code can only be used once.

## Is there a mobile app?

Yes, the TechStore app is available for iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). The app offers all the features of the website: placing orders, tracking deliveries, contacting support, and managing your rewards program. Additionally, the app supports push notifications for order status updates and exclusive discounts.

## Can I buy a gift card?

Yes, gift cards are available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $200. Gift cards are sent electronically to the recipient's email. They are valid for 6 months from the date of purchase. A gift card can be used to pay for any items on the website. If the order total exceeds the gift card value, the remaining balance can be paid using any other payment method.

## What regions do you ship to?

We ship throughout the United States, including Alaska and Hawaii. International shipping is available to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and Australia. Delivery times and costs vary by region. For details, see the "Shipping" section on the website or contact our support team.

## How do I reset my password?

On the login page, click "Forgot Password?" and enter the email address you used during registration. Within 5 minutes, you will receive a password reset link. The link is valid for 24 hours. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Can I cancel an order?

An order can be canceled free of charge before it is handed over to the shipping carrier. To cancel, go to "My Orders" → select the order → click "Cancel." If the order has already been shipped, you will need to process a return after receiving it. Refunds for canceled orders are processed within 1–3 business days to the original payment method.

## How do I change my email or personal information?

Go to your account → "Profile Settings." Here you can update your name, phone number, and default shipping address. To change your email, verification will be required on both the old and new addresses. If you no longer have access to your old email, contact support with your ID for verification.

## How do I view my order history?

Your full order history is available in your account under "My Orders." For each order, you can see: date, items, total, delivery status, and a link to the electronic receipt. You can filter the history by date and status. An export to CSV format is also available.

## Is it safe to enter my card details on the website?

Yes, we use a certified payment gateway compliant with PCI DSS Level 1. Card details are encrypted and are not stored on our servers. All transactions are protected by 3D Secure technology. For added security, we recommend enabling two-factor authentication in your account settings.

## Can I place an order without registering?

Yes, a "Guest Checkout" option is available — you just need to provide your name, phone number, and email. However, without registration, you will not have access to: the rewards program, order tracking in your account, or purchase history. We recommend creating an account for full access to all features.

## Do you work with businesses?

Yes, we offer invoicing and net-30 payment terms for businesses and organizations. To place a corporate order, send your company details to b2b@techstore.com. We will prepare an invoice and provide all necessary documentation. Recurring corporate clients receive special pricing and dedicated account management.
