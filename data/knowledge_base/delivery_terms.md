# Shipping & Delivery Terms

## Shipping Methods

### Courier Delivery (FedEx / UPS)
Door-to-door delivery to the specified address. The courier will contact you approximately 1 hour before delivery for confirmation. You may inspect and test the item upon receipt (up to 15 minutes). Payment to the courier is available via cash or card.

### Pickup Locations
Over 2,000 pickup locations across the United States (partner network). Items are held at the pickup location for 7 days. After the holding period expires, the order is automatically returned to our warehouse, and the customer is refunded. To pick up an order, provide your order number and present a valid ID or the pickup code from your SMS/email notification.

### USPS Shipping
USPS shipping is available for all regions, including Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories. Shipments are sent via Priority Mail or First-Class Package. A tracking number is assigned within 24 hours of dispatch.

## Delivery Timeframes by Region

| Region | Courier (FedEx/UPS) | Pickup Location | USPS |
|--------|---------------------|-----------------|------|
| Major metro areas (NYC, LA, Chicago) | 1–2 business days | 1–2 business days | 3–5 days |
| Other large cities (500K+ population) | 2–3 business days | 2–3 business days | 4–6 days |
| Suburban and mid-size areas | 2–4 business days | 2–4 business days | 5–8 days |
| Rural and remote areas | 3–7 business days | 3–7 business days | 7–14 days |
| Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories | 5–8 business days | 5–8 business days | 10–18 days |

Timeframes are calculated from the moment the order is handed over to the shipping carrier (usually within 1 business day of payment).

## Shipping Costs

### Free Shipping
Shipping is free for orders of $50 or more (any method except express delivery). For TechRewards loyalty program members, the free shipping threshold is reduced to $35.

### Standard Shipping Rates (for orders under $50)
- Courier delivery to major metro areas: $4.99
- Courier delivery to other areas: $6.99
- Pickup location: $2.99
- USPS: $3.99

### Express Delivery
Available in major metro areas. Same-day delivery (for orders placed before 2 PM local time) or next-day delivery. Cost: $9.99 (regardless of order total). For orders over $100, express delivery is $4.99.

## Delivery Tracking

Once the order is handed over to the shipping carrier, you will receive a tracking number via email and SMS. You can track the delivery status:
- In your account under "My Orders"
- Via the direct link in the notification
- In the TechStore mobile app (real-time push notifications)

Delivery statuses: "Order Packed" → "Shipped" → "In Transit" → "Arrived at Local Facility" → "Out for Delivery / Ready for Pickup" → "Delivered."

## Failed Delivery

If the courier is unable to deliver (no one home), a second delivery attempt will be made on the next business day. After 2 failed attempts, the order is redirected to the nearest pickup location, where it will be held for 7 days. You can reschedule delivery for a convenient date via your account or by calling support.

## Oversized and Heavy Items

For items weighing over 70 lbs or exceeding 60 inches on any side, special conditions apply:
- Delivery via courier only (pickup locations not available)
- Carry-up service: free up to the 3rd floor; $10 per additional floor (if no freight elevator is available)
- Shipping cost: $9.99 (not subject to the free shipping threshold)

## International Shipping

International shipping is available to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and Australia. Delivery times: 5–14 business days. Shipping cost is calculated at checkout based on the weight and destination country. Customs duties and import taxes (if applicable) are the responsibility of the recipient.
