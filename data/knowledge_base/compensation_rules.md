# Compensation Rules

## General Principles

Compensation is provided to customers when the store has failed to meet its obligations. The goal of compensation is to retain customer loyalty and make up for any inconvenience caused. Compensation may take the form of reward points, a discount promo code, or a monetary refund.

## Compensation for Delivery Delays

If delivery is delayed beyond the estimated timeframe:
- Delay of 1–2 days: 5% discount promo code for the next order (up to $10 max)
- Delay of 3–5 days: 10% discount promo code for the next order (up to $20 max) + 300 reward points
- Delay of more than 5 days: 15% discount promo code (up to $40 max) + 500 reward points + free express shipping on the next order
- Delay of more than 14 days: customer may cancel the order for a full refund + 1,000 reward points as compensation

Compensation for delivery delays is issued automatically by the support agent without requiring management approval.

## Compensation for Damaged Items

If an item is received with damage:
- Minor packaging damage (item is undamaged): 200 reward points upon customer request
- Item damage (functionality preserved): replacement item + 10% promo code (up to $20 max) OR a 15–20% discount on the current item (customer keeps the item)
- Item completely non-functional: full refund or replacement + 500 reward points + free shipping for the replacement

The customer must provide photos of the damage. Return shipping for damaged items is covered by the store.

## Compensation for Wrong Item Shipped

If the customer receives an incorrect item:
- Free return of the wrong item (courier pickup or prepaid return label)
- Priority shipment of the correct item (express delivery at the store's expense)
- Compensation: 500 reward points + 10% promo code for the next order (up to $20 max)

If the correct item is out of stock, the customer is offered: waiting for restocking (with additional compensation for the wait) or a full refund + 500 reward points.

## Compensation for Technical Issues

If a technical issue on the website or app caused the customer to be unable to place an order, lose reward points, or experience a double charge:
- Double charge: refund of the erroneous payment within 1 business day + 300 reward points for the inconvenience
- Loss of reward points: full point restoration + 100 additional points
- Unable to place an order due to a glitch: promo code for free shipping on the next order

## Compensation Approval Levels

### Automatic Compensation (support agent can issue independently)
- Promo codes up to $20
- Reward points up to 500
- Free shipping on the next order

### Compensation Requiring Manager Approval
- Promo codes from $20 to $60
- Reward points from 500 to 2,000
- Discount on current item exceeding 15%

### Compensation Requiring Department Head Approval
- Promo codes exceeding $60
- Reward points exceeding 2,000
- Refund of shipping fees in non-standard situations
- Any monetary compensation (not in the form of points or promo codes)

## Maximum Compensation Limits

- Maximum reward points per incident: 2,000 points
- Maximum promo code discount: $60
- Total compensation per customer per calendar month: no more than $100 (in any form)
- If limits are exceeded — escalation to the department head

## When Compensation Is Not Provided

- The customer provided an incorrect shipping address
- The customer was unavailable for the courier twice without prior notice
- Item damage occurred due to the customer's fault after delivery
- The compensation claim window has expired (30 days from the date of the incident)
- The customer has already received compensation for this incident

## Escalation Process

If the support agent cannot resolve the issue within their authority or the customer is not satisfied with the proposed compensation:
1. The agent escalates the case to a senior support agent (resolution within 1 hour)
2. If unresolved — escalation to the department manager (resolution within 4 hours)
3. If unresolved — escalation to the Head of Customer Service (resolution within 1 business day)

When escalating, the customer is informed of the expected resolution time and assigned a priority case number.
