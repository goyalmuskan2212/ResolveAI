# Return and Exchange Policy

## General Return Conditions

Customers may return items in original condition within 30 calendar days of receipt (this exceeds the standard 14-day minimum under consumer protection law). To process a return, the item must be in its original packaging, show no signs of use, and have all tags and labels intact. A receipt or electronic invoice from your account is required.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following categories of items are not eligible for return or exchange if they are in working condition:
- In-ear and canal-type earphones/earbuds (for hygiene reasons, if the packaging has been opened)
- Software with an activated license
- Custom-made or personalized items (engraving, custom PC builds)
- Consumables (cartridges, batteries) with opened packaging
- Prepaid cards and gift cards

## Return Process

1. Go to your account → "My Orders" → select the order → click "Initiate Return"
2. Specify the reason for the return and select the items to return
3. The system will automatically generate a prepaid return shipping label
4. Pack the item and drop it off at the nearest UPS or FedEx location, or schedule a carrier pickup (free for orders over $50; otherwise a $4.99 return shipping fee applies)
5. Once the item is received and inspected at our warehouse, the refund will be initiated automatically

Alternatively, you can return the item in person at any TechStore pickup location by presenting your ID and order number.

## Refund Timelines

Refund timelines depend on the original payment method:
- Credit/debit card: 3–5 business days after return approval
- PayPal: 1–2 business days
- Apple Pay / Google Pay: 1–3 business days
- Cash on delivery: refund via bank transfer within 5–10 business days (bank account details required)
- Business invoice (net-30): 5–7 business days

Processing the return request takes up to 2 business days from the time the item is received at our warehouse.

## Exchanges

Instead of a refund, you may request an exchange for the same item or a different model. If the replacement item costs more, you will need to pay the difference. If the replacement costs less, the difference will be refunded to the original payment method. Exchanges are processed through the same steps as returns, by selecting "Exchange" instead of "Refund."

## Returning Defective Items (Warranty Claims)

If an item has a manufacturing defect or fails during the warranty period, the customer is entitled to:
- Free warranty repair (repair period — up to 30 business days)
- Replacement with a new item of the same model
- Exchange for a different model with a price adjustment
- A full refund

For defective items, returns are accepted throughout the warranty period (12–24 months depending on the product category). Return shipping for defective items is covered by the store. In disputed cases, an independent inspection may be conducted at the store's expense (up to 15 business days).

## Partial Returns

If an order contains multiple items, individual items may be returned separately. Shipping costs are not refunded if at least one item from the order is kept. Reward points earned for the order will be recalculated proportionally.

## Returns on Sale Items

Items purchased at a discount or with a promo code are eligible for return under the standard conditions. The refund will be issued for the amount actually paid (after the discount). A promo code used for the purchase will not be reactivated.
